================================================================================
CafeFlow System Update - October 28, 2026
12oz Exclusive for Hot Beverages, Missing DK Cafe Products Restored,
Removal of 22oz from Coffee, and Dynamic Size Selector
================================================================================

SUMMARY OF IMPLEMENTATION:
This update satisfies the requirement to support 12oz cup size exclusively for hot
beverages (specifically Hot Coffee), restore all missing products from the DK Cafe
menu reference (Americano, Spanish Latte, Caramel Macchiato, Butter Croissant, French Fries),
remove the 22oz cup size completely from all Coffee category beverages, and ensure
smooth dynamic size selection on the POS, checkout, and inventory consumption engine.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * Americano:         12oz Hot: ₱100.00 | 16oz Cold: ₱100.00 | 22oz: None
  * Caffé Latte:       12oz Hot: ₱100.00 | 16oz Cold: ₱100.00 | 22oz: None
  * Spanish Latte:     12oz Hot: ₱145.00 | 16oz Cold: ₱145.00 | 22oz: None
  * White Mocha:       12oz Hot: ₱145.00 | 16oz Cold: ₱145.00 | 22oz: None
  * Caramel Macchiato: 12oz Hot: ₱150.00 | 16oz Cold: ₱150.00 | 22oz: None
  * Salted Caramel:    12oz Hot: ₱150.00 | 16oz Cold: ₱150.00 | 22oz: None
  * Biscoff Latte:     12oz Hot: ₱160.00 | 16oz Cold: ₱160.00 | 22oz: None

- 22oz Removal from Coffee:
  * Purged all 22oz records for products in category 'Coffee' from `product_size_prices`.
  * Set `price_22oz = NULL` for all products in category 'Coffee' in `menu_products`.

    auto-generated for Coffee products.
  * Product edit/add modal dynamically hides 22oz input and displays 12oz Hot and 16oz Cold
    price inputs whenever 'Coffee' is selected.

- Non-Coffee Beverages Untouched:
  * Beverages outside Coffee (Milk Tea, Milk Series, Fruit Tea) continue using
    16oz (Tall) and 22oz (Grande), and do NOT have 12oz Hot options.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. RAW INVENTORY CONSUMPTION LOGIC (12oz Hot vs 16oz Cold)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Centralized Recipe Engine (`core/recipe_engine.php`):
  * `RECIPE_CUP_SIZES['12oz']`:
    - Scale: 0.75
    - Cup Pattern: '12oz Hot cup & lid' (matches Item ID 23)
    - Lid Pattern: null (already included in Item ID 23 '12oz Hot cup & lid')
    - Straw: null (hot drinks do not use straws)
  * Hot Drink Ingredient Omissions:
    - 12oz Hot beverages automatically omit 'Ice' and straws from recipe consumption.
    - Espresso beans and milk scale by 0.75 for 12oz servings.
  * Cold Drink Ingredient Consumption:
    - 16oz Cold beverages consume 1x 16oz cup, 1x Flat lid, 1x Bendable straw, Ice, and beans/milk.
  * Substring Matching Fix:
    - Fixed keyword mapping in `resolve_inventory_item` to match whole word boundaries (`\bice\b`),
      preventing 'Sliced cheese' (which contains substring 'ice') from incorrectly binding to Ice.
  * Stock Evaluation:
    - `evaluate_product_stock_state()` dynamically checks the actual available sizes for each product
      (12oz & 16oz for Coffee; 16oz & 22oz for non-coffee). If one size runs out (e.g. 16oz cup runs out
      while 12oz Hot cup is in stock), the product displays "12oz Hot only" and allows ordering 12oz Hot.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. USER INTERFACE & DYNAMIC SIZE POPOVER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- POS Size Popover (`#sizePopover`):
  * Now dynamically generates `.size-btn` buttons based on the product's actual `p.sizes` array.
  * For Coffee items: renders "12oz Hot" with a hot beverage steam icon, and "16oz Cold" with a cold cup icon.
  * For Non-Coffee items: renders "Tall (16oz)" and "Grande (22oz)".
  * Accurately displays real-time stock state per size: disables out-of-stock sizes with a clear "OUT OF STOCK"
    pill while keeping available sizes orderable.
- Product Catalog Card (`.item`):
  * Dynamic price formatting: computes minimum and maximum price across available sizes.
  * For Coffee products with matching 12oz and 16oz prices (e.g. ₱100.00), displays a clean single price
    instead of "₱100.00 – ₱100.00".
- Product Sheet Modal (`#productSheet`):
  * Dynamic category switcher: switching to 'Coffee' displays 12oz Hot (₱) and 16oz Cold (₱) fields while
    hiding the 22oz field.
  * Switching to other beverage categories displays 16oz Tall (₱) and 22oz Grande (₱) fields.
  * Non-beverage categories hide cup sizes and only display the single fixed price input.
