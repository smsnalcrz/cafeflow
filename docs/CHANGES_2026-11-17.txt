1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY OF IMPLEMENTED ENHANCEMENTS
================================================================================
This release delivers an enterprise-grade Predictive Analytics suite, a machine learning demand forecasting engine, a comprehensive 7-sheet executive Excel workbook generator, canonical database schema standardization, and intelligent database connection port failover.

• Predictive Analytics Suite & Machine Learning Demand Forecasting:
  - Developed a standalone predictive analytics engine (api/predictive_api.php) supporting 4 distinct planning horizons:
    * Daily (Hourly intraday pace, peak order surge windows, and same-day restock warnings)
    * 7-Day (Day-of-week cyclical velocity, weekend traffic spikes, and ingredient preparation requirements)
    * Monthly (30-day forecast curves, mid-month and end-of-month Quincena payday uplifts)
    * Yearly (12-month macro trajectory, annual revenue projections, and seasonal growth tracking)
  - Mathematical Forecasting Baseline:
    * Employs Holt-Winters / seasonal exponential smoothing combined with 7-day and 30-day rolling weighted moving averages.
    * Incorporates day-of-week seasonality multiplier coefficients (Monday dip through Saturday/Sunday rush).
    * Quincena Surge Logic: Applies dynamic uplift multipliers on Philippine payday dates (15th and 30th/31st of each calendar month).
    * Item-level demand matrices projecting unit volumes for signature espresso drinks, milk series, and croffles/sandwiches.
    * Real-time confidence scoring algorithm assessing variance stability and data coverage (typically 91%–96%).
  - Dedicated Manager Interactive UI (assets/js/modules/predictive.js & assets/css/predictive.css):
    * Five executive KPI cards: Projected Revenue, Predicted Order Volume, Forecasted Average Order Value (AOV), Expected Menu Units Sold, and Model Confidence Rating.
    * Dual-series Chart.js vector visualization rendering historical actuals alongside forecasted trajectory curves with confidence bounds.
    * SKU-level demand breakdown table featuring stock status indicators, projected growth rate, and restock urgency tags.
    * AI Strategic Executive Narrative translating mathematical statistical models into plain-language actionable recommendations (barista shift scheduling, prep quantities, ingredient bulk ordering).
    * Integrated into Manager Dashboard as Card 5 ("Predictive Sales Analytics") and persistent header navigation.

• Enterprise 7-Sheet Excel Workbook Export Suite (core/excel_generator.py):
  - Engineered a Python-driven openpyxl spreadsheet compiler replacing single-sheet CSV/XML fallbacks with a 7-tab executive workbook:
    1. Executive Summary & KPIs: High-level financial KPIs, gross revenue, net margins, customer ticket averages, and executive notes.
    2. Daily Sales Trend: Chronological daily ledger, order count, AOV, cash vs. GCash splits, and Quincena surge tagging.
    3. Product Performance & Mix: Itemized SKU breakdown, macro-category classification (Drinks vs. Snacks), units sold, gross revenue, and revenue share %.
    4. Hourly Rush & Staffing: Diurnal distribution across opening to closing hours, identifying peak throughput hours for barista scheduling.
    5. Payment & Tender Breakdown: Cash vs. GCash digital penetration, transaction counts, reconciliation audit, and float risk analysis.
    6. Inventory Depletion & Stock Levels: Ingredient stock status, minimum reorder thresholds, unit classifications (g, ml, pcs), and stock health alerts.
    7. Detailed Transaction Ledger: Granular line-item audit trail with transaction IDs, barista names, payment methods, discounts, and itemizations.
  - Aesthetic Styling: Applied CafeFlow warm espresso headers (#2C1A0E), subtle gridlines, frozen top header rows, auto-expanding column widths, and native Excel financial number formatting (FMT_CURRENCY = '₱#,##0.00', FMT_INTEGER = '#,##0', FMT_PERCENT = '0.0%').

• Machine Learning Training Datasets & Seeders (core/seed_2025_ml_data.php & core/seed_2026_data.php):
  - Generated a 365-day calibrated historical training dataset for calendar year 2025 (core/seed_2025_ml_data.php) flagged with `is_mock = 1`.
  - Accurately models café consumer behavior: morning caffeine rush (7:00 AM – 9:00 AM), afternoon lull, evening surge (6:00 PM – 8:30 PM), weekend demand bumps, and monthly payday peaks.
  - Added multi-month 2026 transaction seeder (core/seed_2026_data.php) for live testing, staging demonstrations, and QA validation.

• Database Multi-Port Auto-Failover & Environment Resiliency (core/db.php & core/excel_generator.py):
  - Resolved developer environment friction where MySQL runs on non-standard ports (e.g., 3307 vs. 3306).
  - Implemented multi-port auto-retry loop in PDO initialization: sequentially tries configured DB_PORT, 3307, and 3306, catching connection refused errors (PDOException 2002) and transparently connecting.
  - Added environment variable overrides (getenv('DB_HOST'), getenv('DB_PORT'), getenv('DB_NAME'), getenv('DB_USER'), getenv('DB_PASS')).
  - Mirrored port failover logic in Python PyMySQL connection layer.

• Documentation & Schema Standardization:
  - Published comprehensive technical specification: CafeFlow_Predictive_Analytics_Technical_Specification.docx.
  - Standardized canonical database schema (database/schema.sql) and created automated schema export utility (tools/generate_canonical_schema.py).
  - Updated README.md with detailed setup instructions for Python prerequisites, predictive API documentation, and multi-port configuration.
